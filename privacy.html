Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026

Santrico LLC ("Santrico," "we," "us," or "our") built Salio to run entirely on your device. This Privacy Policy explains what that means in practice: what data Salio collects, how it's used, and what it never does.

1. What Salio collects

Nothing that leaves your device. Salio has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no backend server. There is nothing to sync, nothing to log in to, and nothing stored anywhere but your phone.

All of the following stay stored locally, on-device, and are never transmitted to Santrico or anyone else:

  • Your alarms and their schedules
  • Your dismiss mission settings and preferences
  • Any reference photos you provide for Custom Object missions

If you delete Salio from your device, this data is deleted with it.

2. Camera use

Salio uses your device's camera solely to complete dismiss missions — Random Object and Custom Object. Photo analysis (object recognition) runs entirely on your device using on-device machine learning. No photo you take in Salio is uploaded, transmitted, or shared with Santrico, a cloud service, or any third party at any point.

Photos taken to complete a mission are discarded from memory immediately after on-device verification and are not retained.

3. Permissions we request

Permission Why Salio asks for it
Camera To complete dismiss missions (Random Object, Custom Object)
Notifications To alert you and ring alarms at their scheduled time
Exact alarms (Android) To schedule alarms precisely and reliably

Salio does not request access to your location, contacts, or microphone, and doesn't need them to function.

4. Third-party services

Salio does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites. If this ever changes — for example, if we add crash reporting to improve reliability — we will update this policy, and the corresponding App Store Privacy Nutrition Label and Google Play Data Safety section, before that change ships.

5. Children's privacy

Salio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone — children or adults — since the app collects no personal information at all. If you believe a child has provided us information in a way not described here, contact us at the email below and we will address it.

6. Changes to this policy

If we change what Salio collects or how it works in a way that affects this policy, we'll update this page and revise the effective date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the app's associated App Store and Google Play privacy disclosures at the same time.
