Why doesn't Uppy have a snooze button?

By design. Uppy's whole purpose is to get you out of bed, and a snooze button undermines that every time. The only ways to stop an alarm are to dismiss it — usually by completing its mission — or to use Emergency Escape. There's no in-between.

My alarm didn't ring

This is almost always a permissions or system-setting issue. Work through this list:

  • Notification permission — make sure Uppy is allowed to send notifications in your device's Settings.
  • Exact alarm permission (Android) — Android 12+ requires a separate "Alarms & reminders" permission. Check Settings → Apps → Uppy → Alarms & reminders.
  • Do Not Disturb / Focus mode (iOS) — some Focus configurations can silence alerts from apps that aren't explicitly allowed. Check Settings → Focus and allow Uppy, or allow alarms through.
  • Battery optimization (Android) — some manufacturers aggressively kill background apps to save battery. Exempt Uppy from battery optimization in Settings → Battery → Uppy.

Why can't I pick a custom alarm sound on iOS?

Uppy's iOS alarms are built on Apple's AlarmKit framework, which doesn't expose Apple's built-in ringtone library to third-party apps — every AlarmKit alarm rings the system default sound. This is a platform limitation, not something Uppy can change. On Android, Uppy includes a full in-app sound picker with no such restriction.

I can't complete my dismiss mission

If a mission genuinely can't be completed — poor lighting, the object isn't available, camera trouble — use Emergency Escape. It requires 100 taps to dismiss the alarm, and that tap count increases each time you use it, so it's meant as a real fallback, not a shortcut. It resets back down after 30 days without being used.

How does Random Object work?

When your alarm rings, Uppy asks you to photograph a random everyday object from a list (like "a pair of shoes" or "something red"). On-device image recognition checks that your photo actually shows the requested object before it lets you dismiss the alarm — the photo is analyzed on your phone and never leaves it.

How does Custom Object work?

Custom Object lets you choose your own object ahead of time — say, your bathroom sink, or your running shoes by the door — so your mission always points you somewhere specific. Uppy learns to recognize that object on-device when you set it up, and checks for it the same way when the alarm rings.
