Wake up.

Salio is an alarm with no snooze button. The only way to stop it is to actually get up — complete a dismiss mission, or take the hard way out.

Why it works

An alarm you can't negotiate with

Every design decision in Salio exists to make dismissing the alarm harder than getting up.

No snooze

Dismissing the alarm — or completing its mission — is the only way to stop it. There is no nine-more-minutes.

Dismiss missions

Snap a photo of a random object, or one you've set up in advance, to prove you're up and looking at the world.

Emergency Escape

A real way out when you truly can't complete a mission — deliberately hard, 100 taps that climb every time you use it.

Private by design

Everything runs on your device. No accounts, no servers, no analytics — your photos never leave your phone.
