1. What Salio is

Salio is an alarm app with no snooze button. Instead of snoozing, dismissing an alarm requires completing a "dismiss mission" (such as photographing a specific or random object) or using Emergency Escape, a high-effort fallback. See the Support page for details on how these work.

Salio is built to ring reliably, including through silent mode and a locked screen. But on iOS in particular, Salio does not use Apple's built-in AlarmKit system alert for its ringing screen and sound, and instead keeps a background audio session alive to trigger its own alarm.   • Operating system updates or changes to background execution rules can affect reliability in ways outside our control.

Salio does not guarantee that any alarm will ring at its scheduled time, at a sufficient volume, or at all. Do not rely on Salio as your sole method of waking up for anything where being late or not waking up carries a serious consequence — a flight, a medical need, an exam, or similar. Keep a backup alarm.

3. Emergency Escape

Emergency Escape is a fallback for when a dismiss mission genuinely cannot be completed. It is intentionally high-effort by design and its cost increases with use, as described on the Support page. It is not an emergency service and should never be relied on as one.
